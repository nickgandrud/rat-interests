Ratio background and some history

Ratio is the name of the party series I run here in the Twin Cities. To me, it has become the most important parts to my musical identity and offerings I have in Minnesota.
The party started in 2023 with the intention of being a one-off thing to book me and some friends since it was a last minute thing to cover over for a friend who couldn't throw their show that day back.

I wanted the party to be called "Ratio" for two reasons:

    • For the first reason, I wanted to initially play with the idea of having the ratio of djs to amount of how long the party will last determine the ratio/aspect ratio of the poster itself. For example, if there were 4 Djs and the party lasted for 4 hours, the aspect ratio of the poster would be 4:4 (i.e a square). You can see that this concept was extremely short-lived after the 2nd or 3rd party lol.
      For the second reason, I wanted the name of the party to be vague and non-descript in an effort to not silo any specific musical dance genre to the entity of the party itself. I find it personally difficult to put a finger on what specific genres I play because tastes are always changing for me (I like house for a few months and then I get bored and like to play techno for a month, and then I get bored and play downtempo music for a bit and on and on...) So why should I try to tag a specific genre on a party that I put effort into when I don’t even have a specific genre that I always play out when I dj?
      I think that this 2nd concept of the party is the main reason why it has lasted and I feel will continue to last in my scope of interest. If I want to throw a party that focuses on house music because I am in a house mood, I will just have friends who I trust play good house music and put them on the same bill and just call it Ratio.
    • As time has gone on, the party itself has been a direct reflection of myself at different periods during my journey as someone who enjoys music and nightlife in the twin cities and someone who's tastes change on a dime.
    After some reflection between now and the last one I threw, I thought it would be important to share/archive each memory I have surrounding each Ratio I threw to keep these important memories a bit more clear to me and anyone that is interested in a passion of mine.

    Posters of Ratio

    Before going into the archive, I thought it would be interesting to give just a little bit of background on the poster layout because I have had more than several people ask me about them and why I made them that way. I got the idea from reading comics and how comics portray movement while reading. I thought it would be an interesting idea to somehow show a mini action in each poster that kinda relates to the vibe of each Ratio. Think of it as me trying to put a gif onto the poster but each frame is pasted throughout. For example on my second poster, I thought it would be clever to incorporate a juggling action in the poster since the djs played multiple short sets throughout the night and juggled the controllers between them. Doesn't make a whole lot of sense looking back at it, but it did then and that's what matters to me.
