Ratio V (April 05 2024)

This 5th one was one of the last parties that would happen at this venue since the lease was expiring. Alot of emotions went into this because i was pretty sure that this was going to be the last time i would throw Ratio anywhere cause i just couldnt see it happen anywhere else at the time. Because of this, this ratio felt like an infliction point: If not a ton of people show up to this potential last one, should i continue or should i call it quits. The music i remember for this was the most varied and the most warped & weird. I remember the crowd being pretty good fortunatly but it had thinned out quickly over the night unfortunately
